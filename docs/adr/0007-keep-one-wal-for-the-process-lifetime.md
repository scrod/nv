# Keep one active WAL per editable root session

Status: accepted.

During ordinary operation NV will not periodically checkpoint `Notes & Settings` or rotate its WAL. One host-local WAL remains the durability record for one editable root-session lifetime; switching roots or completing startup recovery closes that lifetime and creates a fresh WAL only after the successor checkpoint is accepted. After editing stops during quit or ordinary folder switching, NV synchronizes the WAL, settles separate-file writes, and installs at most one complete root-closing checkpoint while positive root continuity permits the old root to accept it. If root continuity becomes unavailable, NV attempts no old-root checkpoint: explicit reopening preserves the unavailable WAL for later association, while a verified **Save Current Collection As…** destination may cover and retire it. On the ordinary path, exact local checkpoint verification authorizes WAL deletion followed by Application Support directory synchronization; provider upload is irrelevant. A provably header-only WAL may be removed durably, and closure skips checkpoint installation when no checkpoint-scoped state changed.

Recovery validates records sequentially and may consume only the complete valid prefix; a torn final record or corrupt interior stops replay and remains preserved. Exact-base WALs replay normally, already-represented WALs require only durable cleanup, and base-mismatched WALs may contribute deterministic nondeletion conflict notes but never overwrite, apply logical deletion, or restore settings. A reserved version-8 `DeletedNoteObject` is the additive exception: every valid positively associated modern WAL unions its retirement identity, while base mismatch suppresses its deletion meaning. LSNs establish order only within one WAL. If several local WALs positively match a root, startup snapshots all of them, preserves order within each, assumes no order across them, and installs one exactly verified recovery checkpoint covering every recovered result before deleting them and creating the new active WAL.

The open WAL follows a verified move or positive authority transition with the continuously held rooted resources and is never copied or duplicated. Its header bookmark remains immutable; the next root-session WAL receives refreshed location data. Startup recovery may install a recovered checkpoint before creating a fresh WAL, and storage-format changes use a controlled restart. NV adds no rotation, retained backlog, cleanup checkpoint, sealing chain, provider upload gate, or routine database-version recovery.

Normative requirements: `START-01`, `DUR-01`, `CLEAN-01`, `ROOT-01`.
